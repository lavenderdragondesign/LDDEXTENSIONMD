POWER USER DEFAULT

Built to save clicks, time, and repetitive work.

LDD Tools adds optional workflow helpers, customization, performance controls, hotkeys, Scout AI tools, and faster design utilities directly around MyDesigns.

Power User is now the default. Prefer a simpler interface? Lite mode is still available anytime inside LDD Tools → Settings.

What’s included

⇧ Drag & Drop UploadDrop design files straight into your MyDesigns workflow. Uploads use your default first slot.
✎ Carousel RenamerSpeed up repetitive design renaming without changing your normal MyDesigns workflow.
✦ Scout AI ToolsStyle Creator, Autofiller, reusable style library, imports, categories, and AI-assisted style creation.
Aa Fonts & ThemesCustomize the LDD/MyDesigns experience with font controls and LDD theme presets.
⌨ HotkeysQuick access to common MyDesigns actions like upscale, background removal, mockups, Canvas, and Vision AI.
⚡ PerformanceSafe through Power User performance modes plus optional TinyMD for aggressive debloating.
▣ Preview & Workflow ToolsOptional productivity helpers designed for large design workflows.
⚙ SettingsShow/hide cosmetic UI elements, control LDD features, switch modes, and reset options whenever you want.

Good to know

LDD Tools does not replace MyDesigns. It layers optional helpers on top of it. For multiple designs going to one listing, use the normal MyDesigns Upload system rather than LDD Drag & Drop.

Performance and cosmetic controls can intentionally change what you see in your browser. Your MyDesigns designs, products, listings, files, and account data are not intentionally deleted by these visual/performance options.
