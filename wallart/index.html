🐉 HOW TO USE

  1. Drag images or folders into the box, or click to select.
  2. Press CREATE WALL ART ZIP.
  3. Wait for the progress bar and download.
  4. Find exports in VERTICAL and HORIZONTAL size folders, and untouched files in ORIGINAL_IMAGES.
  5. Check the exports and scripture before selling or printing.

📦 WHAT THIS DOES

Turns your finished art into a print-ready download set in one click. Each image is sorted into VERTICAL or HORIZONTAL by its shape, then exported in all 6 print ratios at true 300 DPI with the DPI metadata stamped in — ready to upload as your Etsy digital files.

Your untouched originals are kept in ORIGINAL_IMAGES, and the LavenderDragonDesign size guide rides along in every ZIP so buyers know what they're getting.

Everything runs locally in your browser — your art never leaves your machine.

Exports 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 1:1, 11:14 and ISO A4 at 300 DPI. The LavenderDragonDesign size guide is included in every ZIP.

Note: Fit keeps your whole image with white fill where the ratio differs — nothing gets cut. Crop fills each frame edge-to-edge with no white but trims the edges. Upscaling cannot restore missing original detail.
