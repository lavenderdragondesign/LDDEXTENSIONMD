⚠ Performance Mode Notice

Performance modes tune LDD Tools and local interface behavior. More aggressive modes can reduce animations, previews, background work, visual effects, tooltips, or other nonessential interface behavior.

These modes do NOT delete or modify your designs, products, listings, uploads, stores, files, or MyDesigns account data.

⚡ TinyMD — Maximum Performance

TinyMD is a separate advanced override and is not one of the four setup modes. It can be enabled later from Performance and requires its own confirmation.

Good to know

LDD Tools does not replace MyDesigns. It layers optional workflow helpers on top of it. For multiple designs going to one listing, use the normal MyDesigns Upload system rather than LDD Drag & Drop.
